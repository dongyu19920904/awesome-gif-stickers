## Selection

We prefer original authors, official rules and source code. Entries identify outdated methods, available prompts/results and material limitations. Creator examples are attributed to their creators; they are not presented as tests conducted by this repository.

Income accounts are kept separate from advertised prices and marketing claims. No fully substantiated Image 2.6-to-WeChat-to-profitable-sales case has been collected yet.
